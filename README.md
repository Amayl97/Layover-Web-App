# 📸 Layover

> Turn your favorite moments into little polaroid keepsakes.

**Layover** is a cute, interactive web app inspired by the photo booth culture I discovered in South Korea. It lets you turn your own photos into customizable polaroids that you can save as digital memories.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://layoverweb.vercel.app

---

## ✨ Inspiration

I was inspired by the photo booth culture in South Korea, where friends can take fun pictures together and turn those moments into something tangible.

Photo booths like these aren't easily accessible where I live in Pakistan, so I wanted to bring a small part of that experience to the web.

With Layover, you can take your own photos, customize them, and create a little polaroid keepsake from the moment.

---

## What You Can Do

* 📷 Upload your own photos
* 🖼️ Choose from different polaroid layouts
* 🎨 Customize frame colors and designs
* 🧸 Add and move stickers
* ✨ Enjoy interactive animations and effects
* 💾 Save your finished polaroid
* 📥 Download it as a PNG

---

## Built With

* **HTML**
* **CSS**
* **JavaScript**

A big part of the experience was built with CSS animations and styling to make the interface feel playful and tactile.

---

## What I Learned

Building Layover helped me explore CSS beyond basic styling.

* Creating animations with **CSS keyframes**
* Using **clip-path polygons** to create custom shapes
* Using **box shadows** to create depth and a more realistic polaroid look
* Understanding how small visual details can change the feel of an interface

---

## Challenges

One of the main challenges was making the user's customization appear correctly in the final downloadable polaroid while keeping the experience simple and smooth.

I also spent a lot of time experimenting with animations, shapes, shadows, and small interactions to make the app feel more like a physical keepsake rather than a regular web interface.
